# GrowthHikes

### Growth, marketing, and software for ambitious businesses

GrowthHikes is founded by **Vinay Dhankhar**. The organization helps businesses turn scattered marketing and operational work into clear, measurable growth systems.

We solve practical problems such as inconsistent digital presence, slow lead follow-up, disconnected customer conversations, and the cost of building reliable software from scratch.
